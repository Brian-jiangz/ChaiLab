OCEAN DIGITAL TWIN

Ocean digital twin as a catalyst for blue-economy innovation

Prof. Chai and international experts systematically reviewed the core architecture of ocean digital twins and key application scenarios in blue-economy development. Published in National Science Review (2026).

Key Points

  • Four-layer modular architecture: data, models, analysis, visualization & interaction
  • Answers "what is now?", "what next?", and "what if?" operational questions
  • Applications: aquaculture, offshore wind, shipping, disaster prevention, blue carbon
  • Roadmap: unified mathematical framework, hybrid modeling, edge-cloud computing

PALEOCLIMATE & DIGITAL EARTH

Paleoclimate and the Digital Earth

Reconstruct the past, simulate the present, foresee the future. Earth system modeling of key periods such as the Last Interglacial to understand the long-term evolution of the carbon cycle, building toward a digital earth of the climate system.
