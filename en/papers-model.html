LOVECLIM-CoSiNE v1.4

LOVECLIM-CoSiNE v1.4: A coupled climate–ecosystem model for paleoceanography

The LOVECLIM earth system model coupled with the CoSiNE marine ecosystem–biogeochemistry module, developed for paleoclimate and paleoceanography simulations such as the Last Interglacial and long-term carbon cycle evolution.

Model Features

  • Coupled LOVECLIM climate model with the CoSiNE ecosystem–biogeochemistry module
  • Designed for paleoclimate simulations and long-term carbon cycle evolution
  • Supports key periods such as the Last Interglacial
  • Global ocean biogeochemistry with ecosystem dynamics

CESM-CoSiNE

CESM-CoSiNE: An Ocean Ecosystem–Biogeochemistry Module Embedded in CESM

CoSiNE (Carbon, Silicon, Nitrogen Ecosystem) is developed and maintained by our group and has been embedded in the CESM Earth System Model (POP2 ocean component) to study the evolution of planktonic ecosystems and the marine carbon cycle at global and regional scales.

Model Features

  • 16 CoSiNE tracers (nutrients, phytoplankton functional groups, zooplankton, DOC, etc.)
  • V2 extends to 22 tracers with carbon isotope (¹³C / ¹⁴C) tracking capability
  • Supports present-day climate, short-term forcing assessments, and paleoclimate (e.g., Last Interglacial) simulations
  • Outputs global and regional (North Pacific, South China Sea) carbon flux, nutrient, and ecosystem structure diagnostics

Selected Work

  • CESM-CoSiNE16: An ocean ecosystem–biogeochemistry module embedded in CESM and its short-term forcing assessment
  • CESM CoSiNE16+5 (CoSiNE22) coupling: expansion of carbon isotope tracers and implementation
