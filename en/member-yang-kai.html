Research TopicRESEARCH TOPIC

My research focuses on the relationship between physical ocean processes and marine ecosystems and carbon cycling, using satellite remote sensing, in-situ observations, and reanalysis data to analyze the impacts of ocean fronts, eddies, and related meso/submesoscale dynamics on phytoplankton distribution, primary productivity, ocean–atmosphere CO2 exchange, and carbon export.

  • Ocean meso/submesoscale processes
  • Marine ecosystem and carbon cycling responses
  • Satellite remote sensing and multi-source ocean observations
