CARBON CYCLE & CLIMATE MODELING
01

Marine Carbon Cycle and Climate Modeling

Developing and improving coupled marine ecosystem–biogeochemistry models such as CESM-CoSiNE to quantify the ocean’s role in regulating atmospheric CO₂, biological pump efficiency, and the response and feedback of the marine carbon cycle to climate change.

  • Development and evaluation of the CESM-CoSiNE coupled ecosystem–biogeochemistry model
  • Biological carbon pump and deep-sea carbon export efficiency
  • Regulation of air–sea CO₂ exchange and pCO₂ patterns by ENSO and other climate modes

OCEAN DIGITAL TWIN
02

Ocean Digital Twin

Building ocean digital twin systems that integrate observation–simulation–prediction into a unified framework, empowering blue-economy innovation.

  • Core architecture of ocean digital twins
  • Key application scenarios in blue-economy development
  • Integrated ocean observation–simulation–prediction
Digital Twin →
PALEOCLIMATE & PALEOCEANOGRAPHY
03

Paleoclimate and Paleoceanography

Earth system modeling of key periods such as the Last Interglacial to understand the long-term evolution of the carbon cycle and climate feedback mechanisms.

  • Simulation of marine environment and carbon cycle during the Last Interglacial
  • LOVECLIM-CoSiNE paleo climate–ecosystem model
  • Integration of paleoclimate proxies and model results
Publications →
OCEAN OBSERVATION & ECOSYSTEMS
04

Ocean Observation and Ecosystems

Monitoring high-frequency variations of nutrients, oxygen, pH, and particulate organic carbon flux with Biogeochemical-Argo floats, and assessing the impacts of climate change on fisheries resources with ecological models.

  • Biogeochemical-Argo high-frequency profiling
  • Deep-sea particulate organic carbon flux monitoring
  • Fisheries resource assessment under climate change
Data →
MARINE HEATWAVES & MODE WATER
05

Marine Heatwaves and Mode Water

Investigating the mechanisms and evolution of marine heatwaves and Subtropical Mode Water and their impacts on marine ecosystems and the carbon cycle.

  • Mechanisms and evolution of marine heatwaves
  • Formation and transport of North Pacific Subtropical Mode Water
  • Impacts of extreme ocean events on ecosystems and the carbon cycle
Publications →
OCEAN MODEL DEVELOPMENT
06

Ocean Model Development

Developing and maintaining the CESM-CoSiNE marine ecosystem–biogeochemistry module, embedded in the CESM Earth System Model (POP2 ocean component), supporting modern-climate and paleoclimate simulations at global and regional scales.

  • CESM-CoSiNE16 embedded in CESM
  • CoSiNE22 extension with carbon isotope tracers
  • Support for modern-climate and paleoclimate simulations
