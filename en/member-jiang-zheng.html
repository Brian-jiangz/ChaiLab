GROUP MEMBER

Jiang Zheng

PhD Student (Class of 2025)
  • Email：jiangz@stu.xmu.edu.cn
  • Research Direction：Earth system model development, paleoclimate simulation, and marine carbon cycle
  • Education：Since 2025, School of Ocean and Earth Sciences, Xiamen University

Research Directions

  • Development and evaluation of the CESM–CoSiNE coupled ocean ecosystem–biogeochemistry model
  • Simulation of marine environment and carbon cycle during typical paleoclimate periods such as the Last Interglacial
  • Mechanisms of ENSO, PDO, and other climate modes in regulating air–sea CO₂ exchange and surface pCO₂ patterns in the equatorial Pacific

PUBLICATIONS
